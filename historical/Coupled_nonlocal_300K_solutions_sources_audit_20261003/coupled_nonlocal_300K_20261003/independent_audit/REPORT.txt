Independent mathematical and numerical audit
Coupled nonlocal device, 300 K, reviewed 2026-10-03 UTC

Conclusion
The implemented bounded dark-device closure is internally consistent with a
one-level Fermi-Dirac reservoir DOS, vacancy-blocked lattice-gas transport,
two independent orientation subpopulations of midpoint traps, and sequential
reversible Marcus transfers. No blocking sign, trap-count, Poisson deposition,
source-conservation, or Jacobian defect was found for the tested envelope.
This is a numerical model audit, not material calibration or validation of a
uniquely identified tunneling mechanism.

Mathematical sign and energy review
Electron energies are EH=-phiH, Et=Eg/2-phit and EL=Eg-phiL. Forward transfer
increments are Et-EH and EL-Et; their sum is Eg-(phiL-phiH). The electrostatic
work is already included. No extra field-lowering energy may be added to these
same increments without specifying a distinct physical mechanism.

The inherited variables give muL=contact_barrier+V+Vt*z_n and
muH=contact_barrier-Vt*z_p. muH is a HOMO electron chemical potential, not a
hole-energy potential. Local chemical splitting is V+Vt*(z_n+z_p). Each
reaction cycle has generation affinity (muH(xH)-muL(xL))/Vt. Generation-positive
Sn and Sp, trap charge, and the cumulative internal conventional current have
consistent signs. Internal current must be included in the local total-current
conservation diagnostic; it is zero at contacts and is not a new terminal
current offset.

For dark stationary operation, V*Jterminal equals the sum of transport,
reaction, and bimolecular free-energy dissipation. The implemented reaction
bath and reservoir heat terms sum to reaction free-energy loss. This identity
does not solve a temperature or heat-transport equation. Illumination would
require a photogeneration/free-energy term before using the same global ledger.

Interpretation and boundaries
- Dividing the MB SG expression by both endpoint activity denominators is a
  legitimate exclusion-hopping discretization. Its continuum electron flux is
  Jn=q*mu*[Vt*dn/dx-n*(1-n/Nc)*dphi/dx]. The inherited mobility is a dilute
  hopping mobility. This is not the conventional fixed-mobility FD drift law
  with conductivity q*mu*n and a generalized Einstein diffusivity.
- The bimolecular law includes both occupancy denominators. Equivalently it is
  beta*[n*p-ni_MB^2*(1-n/Nc)*(1-p/Nc)], so equilibrium and vacancy factors match
  the finite-state reservoirs.
- Each endpoint has one spectral state per trap; this is a declared coupling
  normalization, not an experimentally inferred bulk coupling volume.
- Each orientation is an independent Nt/2 trap subpopulation, with its own
  occupation. One trap coupled simultaneously to both orientation channels
  would be a different kinetic network and needs aggregate-rate elimination.
- The inherited charge reference +q/2 for an empty trap is a compatibility
  background choice, not an identified donor/acceptor charge assignment.
- A reported distance of 1 nm is each H-to-trap or trap-to-L leg. H-to-L endpoint
  separation is 2 nm. Both controls keep the same quadrature weights/count.
- Weights integrate the nominal trap-support interval [2,98] nm exactly.
  Partially overlapped cells retain their grid-node quadrature representative;
  thus representatives may lie slightly outside the nominal support edge.
  This is a mesh quadrature approximation, not a change in total trap count.
- Finite-endpoint geometry must resolve exclusively on interior transport nodes.
  The added geometry guard rejects configurations that would deposit sources
  on contacts or extrapolate outside the resolved interval.
- The code still evaluates ordinary exponentials and rate products. The tested
  voltage envelope is finite and succeeds, but arbitrary extreme iterates may
  overflow or underflow. General-purpose unbounded robustness is not claimed.
- The zero-distance FD Marcus model is its matched physical control. The old
  MB-SRH baseline is a separate regression. Their densities, transport closure,
  and chosen microscopic rates differ, so they should not be described as an
  exact parameter-identical baseline pair.
