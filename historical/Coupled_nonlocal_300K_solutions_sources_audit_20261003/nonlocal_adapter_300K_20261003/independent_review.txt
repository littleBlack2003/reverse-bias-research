Independent numerical review: nonlocal reaction adapter
Reviewed 2026-10-03 (UTC)

Scope and conclusion
Reviewed adapter.py, rates.py, the adapter/rate tests, and the stated assembly
conventions. Final independent run: 41/41 tests pass (18 adapter + 23 rate).
No unresolved blocking defect was found within this bounded reaction-block
scope. This is numerical verification, not identification of a material
mechanism or validation of a coupled device solution.

Independent checks beyond the supplied suite
- The standalone geometric-grid example also ran successfully: normalized
  local charge defect 3.997e-16 and consistent condensed dimensions.
- Randomized nonuniform grids, three overlapping reactions, arbitrary endpoint
  order and widely differing rates/counts: relative cellwise charge-continuity
  defects <=2.55e-15 transient and <=4.34e-16 stationary (40 cases each).
- Separate full-state central differences on a two-reaction spectral example:
  maximum row-normalized Jacobian discrepancies 5.16e-9 transient and 2.44e-8
  stationary. No derivative appeared outside the declared sparse pattern.
- Common gauge shifts preserved sources/current to approximately 4e-15 relative.
- Boundary-node interpolation, non-midpoint nodes and affine-field reproduction
  passed. Node-aligned FV currents agreed with the independent oriented
  point-path current convention.
- Occupancy dynamics pointed into [0,1] at both endpoints. Four thousand exact
  fixed-rate time advances with log rates spanning [-600,600] stayed in [0,1].
- Condensed steady residual and Jacobian have dimensions 3N and 3N-by-3N, and
  equal the corresponding field block of the full steady API exactly.

Defects found and corrected during review
1. Unpaired five-point differences produced spurious port derivatives for
   constant rates. Paired differences now cancel constants exactly, with a
   regression; transient trap charge also has analytic zero port derivatives.
2. Nonfinite statistics inputs formerly passed the convenience mapping.
   Explicit finite checks now reject them.
3. The initial condensed helper returned an unreduced residual vector with a
   reduced Jacobian. Its vector is now reduced consistently and regression-tested.
4. A brittle one-ratio refinement threshold was replaced by seven mesh levels,
   an analytic interpolation-error upper-bound check, and final accuracy plus
   overall reduction checks. Intermediate errors are honestly nonmonotonic.

Interpretation and remaining validation boundaries
The signs of electron/hole sources, trap charging and oriented internal current
are mutually consistent. Shape interpolation/deposition preserves particle
counts and first moments on nonuniform cells. Finite-distance reactions retain
an internal current; it is not an independent constant terminal-current offset.
The zero-distance fixed-rate control reproduces the local SRH convention.

The returned Jacobian is a reaction-source/charge block. Full transport,
Poisson, contacts, variable/residual scaling, consistent DOS-to-carrier
statistics, path-count normalization, and displacement current remain the
responsibility of a future device assembly. A stationary device solve should
use the condensed fields rather than retain zero occupancy equations.
The tests do not establish spectral/path quadrature convergence, contact
behavior, material parameters, device J-V/onset predictions, or robustness for
all representable finite inputs; exponentiated physical rates still must fit
floating-point range. The conditional sequential Marcus kernel is not proof
of a uniquely identified TAT mechanism.
